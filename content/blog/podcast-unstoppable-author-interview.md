**Isaac Russell was interviewed for The Patrick and Mary Unstoppable Author Interview Series about his book, *Improving Profits Through People*.** The book captures the philosophy behind more than three decades of work with organizations like Coca-Cola bottlers, Tyson Foods, and Johnsonville: organizations become stronger and more profitable when they invest in their people.

## About the book

*Improving Profits Through People* draws on Isaac's journey from hourly factory worker to trusted advisor for executives. It focuses on the connection that many organizations miss: the way people are led shows up directly in turnover, productivity, quality, and profit.

## Core ideas

- **People are the profit lever.** Equipment and processes matter, but people determine whether they deliver.
- **Turnover is expensive and fixable.** Most of it traces back to how people are led. ([Three Important Facts About Turnover](/blog/three-important-facts-about-turnover))
- **Frontline leaders make the difference.** Supervisors who set clear expectations and treat people with respect keep their teams.
- **Don't chase symptoms.** Find the [root cause](/blog/the-root-cause-to-lost-profits) and address it.

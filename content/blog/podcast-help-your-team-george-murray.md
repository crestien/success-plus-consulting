## Why helping your team is a leader's real job

Isaac's career started as an hourly factory worker. That experience shaped a belief that runs through all of his work: people genuinely want to succeed, and when they don't, the cause is usually something leadership can fix. Unclear expectations, a lack of recognition, and supervisors who were never taught to lead people show up again and again behind turnover and missed goals.

## The ideas behind Isaac's approach

- **Set clear expectations.** People can't hit a target they can't see.
- **Hold people accountable, respectfully.** Accountability and respect work together, not against each other.
- **Recognize good work.** It's the number one thing employees say their leaders fail to do. ([Read more](/blog/the-number-one-complaint-employees-have-about-their-leaders))
- **Coach on the job.** Lasting change happens in the work, not in a classroom.
- **Look for root causes.** Turnover and low performance are symptoms. Find what's behind them.

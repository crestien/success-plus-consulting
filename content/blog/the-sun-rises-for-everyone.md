To take full advantage of each day, it provides a fresh start from your week or yesterday that may have not been successful. I will always remember a discussion I had with the owner of Mrs. Ressler’s food products. Mrs. Ressler was a holocaust survivor her story left a permanent impression on me. She said, “I don’t forget about the past, but I don’t live in the past”, and this stuck with me. Good leaders understand the importance of a new start, it happens daily with processes and people. The sun rose today for everyone, and brings us the potential that living for today can offer. Are you living in the past, or pushing into the future?
